# Stardew Valley — Year 1 Community Center Checklist

A season-by-season plan for finishing the Community Center in Year 1 of Stardew Valley 1.6, built from the [Bundles wiki page](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Bundles). That's 30 bundles in 6 rooms.

## Before you start

This plan assumes two settings from the **Advanced Options** when you create the save. You can't change them later.

- **Community Center Bundles: Normal.** It does **not** cover [Remixed Bundles](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Remixed_Bundles), which swap in different items.
- **Guarantee Year 1 Completable: on.** Red Cabbage Seeds aren't sold by Pierre until Year 2. With this option on, the [Traveling Cart](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Traveling_Cart#Guaranteed_Red_Cabbage) is guaranteed to sell them on **one random visit** between Spring 7 and Winter 16 (the Night Market). Without it, Red Cabbage in Year 1 is down to luck at the cart.

> 🥬 The guarantee only applies to that one cart visit, so don't skip a Friday or Sunday, and buy the seeds the first time you see them.

## Ground rules

- **Collect in season, turn in whenever.** Items never spoil, so keep a "CC chest" and put one of every bundle item in it. The dates below are the last day you can **get** each item, not the last day you can hand it in.
- **Start the Community Center early.** From Spring 5, enter town from the Bus Stop between 8am and 1pm on a dry day, then follow the [unlock steps](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Community_Center) to meet the Wizard.
- **Finish the Vault before the Specialty Fish Bundle.** Sandfish only lives in the Desert, and the bus to the Desert gets fixed when the Vault is done. The Traveling Cart is the only other source.
- **Check the [Traveling Cart](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Traveling_Cart) every Friday and Sunday** for the guaranteed Red Cabbage Seeds and backups for the hard items (Truffle, Rabbit's Foot, Duck Feather, Wine, Sandfish). It's also at the Night Market (Winter 15–17).
- **Save a spare of every item two bundles need:**
  - Maple Syrup: Exotic Foraging (optional) **and** Chef's
  - Oak Resin: Exotic Foraging (optional) **and** Enchanter's
  - Red Mushroom: Exotic Foraging (optional) **and** Dye
  - Purple Mushroom: Exotic Foraging (optional) **and** Field Research
  - Apple: Artisan (optional) **and** Fodder (needs 3)
  - Pomegranate: Artisan (optional) **and** Enchanter's
  - Parsnip, Melon, Pumpkin, Corn: each crop bundle **and** Quality Crops (5 gold)
- **[The Farm Cave](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/The_Cave):** fruit bats are a nice bonus, but they rarely drop tree fruit. **Don't count on them for Apples or Pomegranates; plant the trees.**

### Fruit trees

Plant [Apple](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Apple_Tree) and [Pomegranate](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Pomegranate_Tree) saplings by **Summer 1** so they fruit from Fall 1. They take 28 days and need a clear 3×3 space.

### Quality Crops strategy (any 3 of the 4: 5 gold each of Parsnip, Melon, Pumpkin, Corn)

- **Corn is the easiest.** Every regrow harvest gets its own chance at gold, and your Farming level will be higher by Fall. Plant a lot of it with fertilizer early in Summer.
- **Melon and Pumpkin:** plant extras with fertilizer.
- **Parsnip** is your backup. Gold is hard at Spring farming levels, but if you plant lots of fertilized parsnips early, you might get 5.
- **[Basic Fertilizer](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Basic_Fertilizer):** craft it at Farming 1, or buy it from Pierre from Spring 15. Its wiki page has the gold chance at each Farming level.
- A rough target from a Steam year-1 guide: about **30 Parsnips, 30 Melons, and 8 Corn**, all with Basic Fertilizer.

### Greenhouse backup

When you finish the Pantry, the Greenhouse is fixed. You can still grow Red Cabbage, Poppy, Sunflower, and Wheat there in Winter if you missed them outside. This matters most if the Red Cabbage Seeds don't show up at the cart until after Summer 19.

| Deluxe Coop + [rabbit](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Rabbit)                   | 20,000g + 8,000g         | **Rabbit's Foot** (Enchanter's)                            | Early Fall (or hope the cart has a Rabbit's Foot)               |

> 🐔 Marnie picks chicken colors at random but shows the color before you pay, so cancel and retry to get one white and one brown. Animals only make Large products (and Duck Feathers or Rabbit's Feet) once they like you, so buy them early and pet them every day.

> 💰 This whole plan costs about **180,000g** plus seeds: roughly 13k in Spring, 56k in Summer, 69k in Fall, and the 42.5k Vault in Winter. The buildings also need about **3,000 Wood and 1,100 Stone**; in Year 1 Robin sells Wood for 10g and Stone for 20g if you run short. Each season below ends with money tips.

- You can pay the smaller Vault bundles as soon as the Vault opens, so you don't have to save all 42,500g at once.
